CourseFlow System Architecture & Viva Guide
1. System Purpose & NBA Accreditation Alignment

CourseFlow is a university Course File Management System (CFMS) designed to automate, standardize, and audit all documentation mandated by the National Board of Accreditation (NBA Tier-1) and Outcome-Based Education (OBE) frameworks.

2. Key Functional Workflows
  • Course File 10-Point Checklist: Tracks Course Info, Syllabus, Session Plans, Students, Assessments, Marks, Attendance, Feedback, CO-PO Mapping, and Attainment.
  • Outcome Attainment Calculation: Direct Attainment (Internal CIE 30% + External SEE 70%) combined with Indirect Attainment (Exit Survey 20%) to measure CO-PO correlation.
  • Role-Based Access: Faculty manage teaching records and generate course dossiers; Administrators oversee faculty course allocations and monitor departmental compliance.
